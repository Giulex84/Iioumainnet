# Interface localization

Verified in i18n.js at Mainnet baseline 76b8d52bae67bb6ad679dacb7927748b3cd1a3f6 on 2026-10-31: English, Italian, Simplified Chinese (zh-CN), Arabic, Vietnamese, Korean, Indonesian and Nigerian Pidgin (pcm) are offered by the selector.

The preference is kept locally under iiou-language. The interface includes Arabic direction handling. The selector alone does not certify complete translation coverage or native-speaker quality. User-entered agreement text is excluded from translation; network, payment amounts and stored agreement data must remain unchanged.

Canonical legal pages remain in English. Mainnet and Testnet retain separate SDK mode, deployment, credentials and storage. No Testnet alignment is performed by this documentation-only change.
